# CLA acknowledgement template (forthcoming)

This document will be a fillable template that contributors may
use, in addition to submitting a pull request, to acknowledge the
terms of [`CLA.md`](CLA.md) in writing. The maintainer may request
this for material Contributions (more than 100 lines of code, or
any change to a security-critical component, or any change to the
legal docs in this directory).

Until this template is published, the CLA is accepted by conduct
under §10 of [`CLA.md`](CLA.md).
